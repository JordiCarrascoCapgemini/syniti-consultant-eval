05 Competency ladder
This is the full competency matrix - the expected rating for every competency at every C-level, with the Career Framework role each level represents. The level selected in section 01 is highlighted; use it to see what the next level expects.
How to read: rows are competencies grouped by area; columns are career levels with their Career Framework roles; each cell is the expected proficiency at that level. Read a row left to right to see how the requirement grows as someone progresses. The highlighted column is the level currently selected in section 01.
