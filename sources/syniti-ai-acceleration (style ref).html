Link 05 · Delivery

The real shift in delivery is who can do the work.

Senior-quality work, from less experienced people

The method is built into the work itself, so a less experienced consultant produces what used to need a senior — at the same quality. Good output stops depending on who happens to be assigned.

Same work, better margin

When a C2 delivers what previously needed a C4, the cost of delivering the engagement falls while the price doesn't. That gap is margin — a significant improver across a portfolio of work.
