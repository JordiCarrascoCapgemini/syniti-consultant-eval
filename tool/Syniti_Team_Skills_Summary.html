Team Skills Summary

See every consultant's skills, side by side.

Import the evaluation files exported from the Skills Evaluation and Enablement tool - one per consultant, as many as you like - and this tool merges them into team KPIs, a per-consultant roster, a heatmap across all thirteen competency areas, and a drill-down for anyone on the team. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

04 Skills heatmap
Each cell is the consultant's average rating minus the average expectation for that area, at their current level. Click a name or a cell to open the drill-down.
Positive (green) means the consultant is at or above expectation in that area on average; amber is a small gap; red is a material gap of a full point or more. Grey cells mean the area was not scoped, not rated, or the consultant's level on that evaluation is not recognised.
05 Team growth by area
Compares each consultant's earliest and latest imported evaluation, area by area, and averages the movement across the team.
This compares each consultant's earliest and latest imported evaluation, area by area, then averages the movement across the team. Movement is measured against the expectation at the time of each evaluation, so it stays fair across promotions - a consultant who was promoted mid-series is still compared to what was expected of them at each point. Areas with no repeat evaluations are not shown.
